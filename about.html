About

Brooklyn-based. New York-licensed. Built for the neighborhood.

We started CamerasNYC because the security industry treats homeowners and small businesses like a quarterly target. Every install we do, we do as if we'll see the customer again — because, usually, we will.

Origin

How this got started.

Marcus Chen spent 12 years installing low-voltage and security systems for one of the major national security firms across the five boroughs. The work was good. The customer experience was not.

Calls went to a help line in another state. Service techs rotated every visit. Customers were sold three-year monitoring contracts they didn't understand for systems they didn't fully control. So in 2014, we left and started CamerasNYC with three rules: we own the relationship, we own the install, and we own the support.

We've installed 500+ systems since. Some customers we've serviced for five years and counting. Most of our work comes from neighbors of customers — the strongest signal we know that we're doing this right.

What's different

Three things the chains don't do.

01

You own the system.

No 36-month monitoring contracts. No equipment leased to you. You own every camera, every cable, every byte of footage. If you stop paying us anything, the system still works.

02

Real phone, real person.

One number. Goes to a person who has been on your roof. No call routing, no tier-one script, no "we'll have to escalate that." We pick up — and if we can't, we call back the same day.

03

Pricing that holds.

The quote is the price. We don't add change orders for "additional drilling" or "longer cable runs" — those are in the original walk-through. If we miss something, we eat it.
